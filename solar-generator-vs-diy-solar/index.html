Buying guide

Solar Generator vs DIY Solar Kit

Portable power stations are simple. DIY component systems are more flexible. The right choice depends on load size, upgrade plans, and how permanent the installation should be.

Choose a solar generator when…

  • You want a simple plug-and-play backup solution.
  • Your loads are modest: fridge, lights, devices, router, small appliances.
  • You value portability more than maximum expandability.

Choose a DIY kit when…

  • You need larger battery storage or a 48V system.
  • You want to power pumps, tools, AC, or cabin/home circuits.
  • You want to upgrade panels, batteries, and inverter separately over time.
