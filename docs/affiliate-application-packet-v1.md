## Short site description

SolarKitGuide.com is a beginner-friendly solar planning site that helps homeowners, RV owners, cabin owners, and DIY off-grid buyers estimate solar panel wattage, battery storage, inverter size, and system type before comparing equipment.

The site combines free solar calculators with plain-English guides covering panels, batteries, inverters, solar generators, off-grid kits, backup power, and equipment selection.

## Stronger application blurb

SolarKitGuide.com helps US consumers plan solar purchases before contacting a retailer or buying equipment. The site combines free sizing calculators with practical guides for home backup, off-grid cabins, RV/camper systems, battery banks, inverter sizing, and solar generator vs component-based DIY systems.

Our recommendation process starts with sizing logic first: daily energy needs, surge loads, battery autonomy, inverter capacity, panel wattage, system voltage, and use case. Product links are then matched to the resulting equipment category rather than pushing one-size-fits-all products.

We are especially interested in partners with US availability, clear warranties, dependable fulfillment, deep links/product feeds, and catalogs that include solar panels, batteries, inverters, charge controllers, complete kits, and backup-power systems.
