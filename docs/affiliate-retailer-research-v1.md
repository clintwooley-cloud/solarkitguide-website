# SolarKitGuide affiliate retailer research v1

Date: 2026-05-06

Goal: find USA-focused solar product retailers/brands suitable for SolarKitGuide affiliate monetization, preferably with US/local warehousing and DIY-friendly catalogs.

## Strong candidates

### A1 SolarStore
- URL: https://a1solarstore.com/affiliate-program.html
- Fit: solar panels, batteries, inverters, charge controllers, PV system equipment.
- Affiliate terms found: 6% commission; 180-day cookie; US geo-targeting; average bill examples; commission cap noted at $250/transaction.
- Useful extras: product feed, dynamic iframe product block, custom API integration.
- Notes: strong fit for product-comparison/content pages and calculator result pages.

### Signature Solar
- URL: https://signaturesolar.com/affiliate-program
- Fit: very strong for DIY/home backup/off-grid; EG4 batteries/inverters, solar panels, kits/bundles, Victron systems, hybrid/grid-tie/off-grid kits.
- Affiliate terms found: affiliate program exists; public page promotes competitive/high commissions and dedicated support, but exact public commission rate was not clearly extracted.
- USA signal: Texas-based team mentioned on partner pages; USA-based solar experts noted.
- Notes: probably one of the most strategically important partners because order values can be large and products map directly to calculator outputs.

### Current Connected
- URL: https://www.currentconnected.com/affiliates/
- Fit: renewable energy products, solar panels, batteries, inverters, charge controllers, generators, racking, cable, tools; brands include Victron, Canadian Solar, Cummins, IntegraRack, etc.
- Affiliate terms found: average active affiliate earns around 8%; monthly payouts; US/Canada applicants; every product eligible. Minimum payout $1,000 US / $1,500 outside US. Requires active relevant content and 12 credited transactions in 12 months to maintain eligibility.
- Notes: better once SolarKitGuide has visible content; may not approve a brand-new empty site.

### Renogy US
- URL: https://www.renogy.com/pages/affiliate-program
- Fit: RV/camper/off-grid beginner market; solar kits, batteries, panels, charge controllers, inverters.
- Affiliate terms found: program exists. Search snippets found “minimum 6%” on Renogy DE; US page extraction did not clearly expose terms.
- Notes: very good for beginner-friendly kits, RV, van, cabin audiences.

### EcoFlow US
- URL: https://us.ecoflow.com/pages/affiliate-program
- Fit: portable power stations, solar generators, home backup, smart batteries.
- Affiliate terms found: minimum 5% commission on qualifying purchases from search result/page snippets.
- Notes: excellent for backup-power path and “solar generator vs DIY system” content.

### BLUETTI US
- URL: https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/59271
- Fit: portable power stations and home backup systems.
- Affiliate terms found: 5%–10% tiered commission; 30-day cookie; high AOV around $1,000; Awin network.
- Notes: strong competitor/comparison content opportunity vs EcoFlow/Jackery/Anker.

### Anker SOLIX
- URL: https://www.ankersolix.com/become-an-affiliate
- Fit: portable power stations, home solar energy storage, smart solar solutions.
- Affiliate terms found: 5% commission; 30-day cookie; affiliate tools and dedicated manager.
- Notes: good fit for backup power, home battery, and portable power station comparisons.

### RICH SOLAR
- URL: https://richsolar.com/pages/affiliate-program
- Fit: off-grid solar kits, RV/camping kits, cabin/tiny-house kits, panels, lithium batteries, inverters, charge controllers, wiring/components.
- Affiliate terms found: program page exists but extracted content did not show commission rate.
- USA/warehouse signal: free fast shipping, in-store pickup; wholesale page references warehouse inventory.
- Notes: excellent product fit for off-grid/RV/cabin path; need direct terms confirmation.

### LiTime
- URL: https://www.litime.com/pages/affiliate-program
- Fit: LiFePO4 batteries, especially 12V/24V/48V budget/mid-market batteries.
- Affiliate terms found: affiliate/creator program exists; AU snippet found up to 5%; US extraction showed local warehouses/free fast shipping but not commission details.
- USA/local warehouse signal: “Free & Fast Shipping from Local Warehouses.”
- Notes: good for battery-bank calculator output and battery comparison pages.

## Additional possible retailers / partners to investigate

- ShopSolar / ShopSolarKits — strong kit catalog; search snippets suggest up to 5% commission, but need direct partner page confirmation.
- GoGreenSolar — strong DIY kit seller; affiliate program not clearly found; may have quote/installer lead opportunities instead.
- AltE Store — respected solar distributor/educator; found installer partner program, not clear consumer affiliate program.
- Unbound Solar — DIY solar design/sales; affiliate program unclear.
- Jackery — portable solar generator brand; third-party sources suggest 5%–8%, but verify official US program.
- Battle Born Batteries — premium US battery brand; affiliate program unclear; possible dealer/partner route.

## Recommendation

Start with a mixed affiliate stack:

1. **Core DIY solar equipment:** Signature Solar, A1 SolarStore, Current Connected.
2. **RV/cabin/beginner kits:** Renogy, RICH SOLAR.
3. **Portable power/home backup:** EcoFlow, BLUETTI, Anker SOLIX, Jackery.
4. **Battery-specific recommendations:** LiTime, possibly Battle Born if partner terms exist.

For launch, prioritize programs that publish clear terms and are likely to approve newer content sites: A1 SolarStore, EcoFlow, BLUETTI, Anker SOLIX, Renogy. Approach Signature Solar, Current Connected, and RICH SOLAR once the first calculator/prototype and 5–10 strong articles are live.
