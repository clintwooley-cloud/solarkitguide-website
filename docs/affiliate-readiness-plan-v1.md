## Compliance notes

FTC guidance says material connections, including financial relationships, should be disclosed clearly and where people will see them. Disclosures only on an About page, profile page, footer, or behind a click may be missed. Use simple language such as “ad,” “sponsored,” or a plain affiliate commission statement. Do not make product-experience claims unless actually tested/verified.

Operational rules for affiliate links:

- Use `rel="sponsored"` on affiliate links; consider `nofollow` too if desired.
- Put a short disclosure before/near affiliate links or recommendation boxes.
- Do not use copied brand images/copy unless allowed by the program or product feed.
- Avoid paid search/trademark bidding unless explicitly allowed.
- Keep recommendation logic honest and avoid unsupported electrical/performance claims.

## Application blurbs

### Short application blurb

SolarKitGuide.com is a consumer education site focused on helping US homeowners, RV owners, cabin owners, and DIY off-grid buyers size solar equipment before they buy. The core tool is a free solar sizing calculator that estimates panel, battery, inverter, and kit categories based on electric bill, appliance loads, backup-power needs, or off-grid use case.

We publish beginner-friendly calculators, buying guides, product comparisons, and equipment shopping lists. We are looking to promote reputable solar retailers and brands with strong US availability, reliable fulfillment, clear warranties, and products that match real user needs.

### Stronger blurb for selective DIY equipment programs

SolarKitGuide.com helps US consumers plan solar purchases before contacting a retailer or buying equipment. The site combines free sizing calculators with practical guides for home backup, off-grid cabins, RV/camper systems, battery banks, inverter sizing, and solar generator vs component-based DIY systems.

Our recommendation process starts with sizing logic first: daily energy needs, surge loads, battery autonomy, inverter capacity, panel wattage, and use case. Product links are then matched to the resulting equipment category rather than pushing one-size-fits-all products. We are building SolarKitGuide for buyers who want to understand what they need before purchasing, which should send partners better-educated and higher-intent visitors.

We are especially interested in partners with US availability, clear warranties, dependable fulfillment, deep links/product feeds, and catalogs that include solar panels, batteries, inverters, charge controllers, complete kits, and backup-power systems.
