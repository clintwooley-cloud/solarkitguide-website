# SolarKitGuide launch sequence v1

## Objective

Launch SolarKitGuide.com as a free solar calculator + buying guide that can monetize through solar equipment affiliate programs.

## Phase 1 — Foundation

1. Confirm domain/DNS/hosting approach.
2. Build simple brand identity:
   - Name: SolarKitGuide
   - Core promise: size the right solar kit before you buy
   - Tone: practical, beginner-friendly, trustworthy, not hypey
3. Create MVP site structure:
   - Home
   - Calculator
   - Solar kit guide / how it works
   - Affiliate disclosure
   - Privacy policy
   - Contact

## Phase 2 — Calculator MVP

Calculator should start with a simple split:

1. “I know my electric bill”
   - Monthly bill or yearly kWh
   - State/ZIP
   - Desired offset percentage
   - Optional roof/shade questions
   - Result: estimated kW solar array, panels, rough cost/savings, kit category

2. “I know what I want to power”
   - Use case: RV, cabin, tiny home, backup, full off-grid, custom
   - Appliance presets + custom loads
   - Hours/day and duty cycle
   - Days of autonomy
   - Battery chemistry
   - Result: daily kWh, solar array watts, battery kWh/Ah, inverter watts, system voltage

3. “I want backup power”
   - Essentials checklist: fridge, freezer, lights, Wi-Fi, medical device, sump pump, window AC, well pump, etc.
   - Outage duration target
   - Solar recharge yes/no
   - Result: portable power station vs DIY battery/inverter recommendation

## Phase 3 — Affiliate readiness

Before applying broadly:

- Publish affiliate disclosure.
- Publish at least 5 useful articles.
- Have calculator prototype or screenshots live.
- Create about/contact pages so applications look legitimate.
- Apply first to clear-term programs: A1 SolarStore, EcoFlow, BLUETTI, Anker SOLIX, Renogy, LiTime.

## Phase 4 — Starter SEO content

Recommended first 10 pages/articles:

1. Solar Kit Size Calculator
2. Off-Grid Solar Calculator
3. Battery Bank Calculator
4. Inverter Size Calculator
5. Solar Generator vs DIY Solar Kit
6. Best Solar Kits for Cabins
7. Best Solar Kits for RVs
8. How Many Solar Panels Do I Need?
9. What Size Battery Do I Need for Solar?
10. 12V vs 24V vs 48V Solar Systems

## Phase 5 — Product mapping

Create affiliate recommendation tiers:

- Small portable: phones, laptops, lights, camping
- RV/camper: fridge, lights, fans, outlets, optional AC warning
- Cabin starter: fridge, lights, pump, small appliances
- Home backup essentials: fridge/freezer, lights, internet, medical/sump
- Full off-grid: larger 48V system, rack batteries, hybrid/off-grid inverter

Each tier should map to:

- Solar panel wattage range
- Battery kWh range
- Inverter wattage range
- Kit/product categories
- Safety caveats
- Affiliate partner options

## Recommendation

Do not wait for every affiliate account before building. Build the calculator/site first with neutral product categories, then swap in partner links as approvals come in. That keeps momentum and makes affiliate applications stronger.
