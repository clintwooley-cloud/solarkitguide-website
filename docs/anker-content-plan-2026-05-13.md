# Anker SOLIX Content Plan for SolarKitGuide

## Why Anker fits SolarKitGuide

Anker SOLIX is a strong fit for the portable power side of SolarKitGuide because readers already searching for BLUETTI, EcoFlow, Renogy, and solar generators will recognize Anker as a mainstream electronics brand. The best angle is not “Anker is good”; it is practical sizing, runtime math, and whether the SOLIX box actually matches the use case.

Use the same editorial stance as the BLUETTI pages:

- Explain watt-hours vs watts clearly.
- Avoid whole-home backup hype unless the product is actually designed for that path.
- Push readers to run their loads first.
- Use affiliate links, but keep the recommendation grounded in fit.

## Recommended first articles

### 1. Anker SOLIX C1000 Gen 2 review

URL: `/anker-solix-c1000-gen-2-review/`

Working title: **Anker SOLIX C1000 Gen 2 Review: Is a 1,024Wh Power Station Enough for Backup?**

Why first:

- Broadest beginner fit.
- Direct competitor to BLUETTI AC180 / Elite 100 V2 style shoppers.
- Good for camping, RV, outage essentials, refrigerators, routers, fans, laptops, CPAP-style light backup discussion, and small cabin loads.

Specs to verify before publishing:

- 1,024Wh LFP battery capacity.
- 2,000W AC output, 3,000W peak.
- 600W max solar input.
- 49-minute full AC recharge with UltraFast mode enabled in app.
- 10 ms UPS switchover.
- 4,000 cycles to at least 80% capacity.
- Approx. 24.9 lb.
- 10 output ports.

Best positioning:

- “A strong 1kWh-class station when you want high output and fast recharge in a lighter box.”
- Better for selected essentials than full-house backup.
- Solar input is good for this size, but still capped at 600W.

### 2. Anker SOLIX C2000 Gen 2 review

URL: `/anker-solix-c2000-gen-2-review/`

Working title: **Anker SOLIX C2000 Gen 2 Review: 2,048Wh Backup Power Without Jumping to Whole-Home Systems**

Why second:

- Higher-ticket product.
- Better storm-prep and RV angle.
- Natural comparison to BLUETTI Elite 200 V2.
- Can capture “how long will it run a fridge?” search intent.

Specs to verify before publishing:

- 2,048Wh LFP battery capacity.
- 2,400W rated AC output, 4,000W peak.
- 800W max solar input.
- 80% recharge in 45 minutes / 100% in 58 minutes.
- Expandable to 4kWh with BP2000 Gen 2 expansion battery.
- TT-30R RV outlet.
- 10 ms UPS.
- Approx. 41.7 lb.
- 4,000 cycle life.

Best positioning:

- “More meaningful backup than a 1kWh station, but still selected-load backup — not automatic whole-home power.”
- Strong for fridge/freezer/router/fans/lights and RV power.
- Be careful around runtime claims; use ranges and encourage load measurement.
