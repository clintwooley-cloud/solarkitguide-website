# SolarKitGuide affiliate outreach templates

## Short application blurb

SolarKitGuide.com is a new consumer education site focused on helping homeowners, RV owners, cabin owners, and DIY off-grid buyers choose the right solar equipment before they buy. Our core product is a free solar sizing calculator that will recommend appropriate panel, battery, inverter, and kit categories based on a user’s electric bill, appliance load, backup-power needs, or off-grid use case.

We plan to publish beginner-friendly calculators, buying guides, product comparisons, and equipment shopping lists. We are looking for reputable solar retailers and brands with strong US availability, reliable fulfillment, and products that match real user needs.

## Longer application blurb

SolarKitGuide.com is being built as a practical solar planning and buying guide for US consumers. The site will help users answer questions like:

- What size solar system do I need?
- How much battery capacity do I need?
- What size inverter should I buy?
- Should I choose a portable power station, a complete kit, or a component-based DIY system?
- Which equipment fits an RV, cabin, home backup, or off-grid setup?

The main tool will be a free solar calculator with multiple paths: home electric bill / rooftop solar, appliance-based off-grid sizing, backup power sizing, and DIY equipment shopping lists. We will support the calculator with SEO-focused guides, comparison articles, and recommended equipment categories.

We’re interested in promoting partners that offer good product quality, US availability, clear warranties, and a strong customer experience. Our goal is to send buyers who are better educated and closer to purchase because they understand what kind of system they need.

- Are there commission caps per order?
- Do you support deep links to product/category pages?

- Are coupon codes available for content partners?
