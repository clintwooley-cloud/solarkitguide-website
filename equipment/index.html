How to use this page

If you already have a calculator result, look for equipment that matches your estimated solar watts, usable battery capacity, inverter watts, surge needs, and system type. If you do not have a result yet, start with the calculator first.

Buying shortcut: A low price only helps if the kit matches your loads. Compare panel watts, battery capacity, inverter output, and surge rating together.

Affiliate disclosure: Some product links may earn SolarKitGuide a commission at no additional cost to you. Recommendations should be matched to use case and sizing needs, not just the highest commission.

Easiest path

Portable power stations

Best for simple backup, camping, tailgating, device charging, renters, and people who want a plug-and-play box instead of building a component system.

  • Check AC output watts and surge rating.
  • Check usable battery capacity in Wh or kWh.
  • Check maximum solar input before buying panels.

Cabin / RV / shed

Small off-grid kits

Best for lights, fans, routers, DC loads, small refrigerators, water pumps, and modest cabin or RV loads where you want a complete parts list.

  • Match panels to daily energy use and sun hours.
  • Match battery capacity to overnight/cloudy-day needs.
  • Use pure sine wave inverter capacity for AC loads.

Backup power

Essential-load backup

Best for refrigerators, freezer, internet, lights, fans, medical devices, or a few selected circuits during outages.

  • Separate running watts from startup surge watts.
  • Decide whether backup should last hours or days.
  • Confirm transfer/installation requirements before buying.

Hybrid home solar equipment

Best for larger home backup or solar-plus-battery systems where hybrid inverters, 48V batteries, permitting, and professional installation may matter.
