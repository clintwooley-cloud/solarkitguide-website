How to use this page

If you already have a calculator result, use it as a shopping filter. Match your estimated solar watts, usable battery capacity, inverter watts, surge needs, and system type before you compare products. If you do not have a result yet, start with the calculator first so you know what class of gear you should actually be comparing.

Buying shortcut: A low price only helps if the gear matches your loads. Compare panel watts, battery capacity, inverter output, surge rating, and solar input together before you chase a deal.

Easiest path

Portable power stations

Best for simple backup, camping, tailgating, device charging, renters, and buyers who want a plug-and-play option instead of building a full component system.

  • Check AC output watts and surge rating.
  • Check usable battery capacity in Wh or kWh.
  • Check maximum solar input before buying panels.

Cabin / RV / shed

Small off-grid kits

Best for lights, fans, routers, DC loads, small refrigerators, water pumps, and modest cabin or RV loads where you want a more complete parts list and clearer upgrade path.

  • Match panels to daily energy use and sun hours.
  • Match battery capacity to overnight/cloudy-day needs.
  • Use pure sine wave inverter capacity for AC loads.

Backup power

Essential-load backup

Best for refrigerators, freezers, internet, lights, fans, medical devices, or a few selected circuits during outages when runtime and surge capacity matter more than panel count alone.

  • Separate running watts from startup surge watts.
  • Decide whether backup should last hours or days.
  • Confirm transfer/installation requirements before buying.

Larger systems

Hybrid home solar equipment

Best for larger home backup or solar-plus-battery systems where hybrid inverters, 48V batteries, permitting, and professional installation may matter more than bargain shopping.
