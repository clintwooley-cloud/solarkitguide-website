Off-grid sizing

Off-Grid Solar Calculator

Off-grid systems need more than panels. The battery bank, inverter, charge controller, wire size, and surge loads all matter.

Use appliance mode

The off-grid flow

  1. List every appliance you plan to run.
  2. Estimate watts, hours per day, and duty cycle.
  3. Add autonomy days for cloudy weather.
  4. Choose battery chemistry.
  5. Size solar, battery, inverter, and system voltage together.

Watch the hidden loads

Refrigerators, pumps, air conditioners, microwaves, and power tools can have startup surges much higher than their running watts. That surge can determine inverter size.
