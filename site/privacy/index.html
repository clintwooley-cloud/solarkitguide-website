Privacy

Privacy, in plain English

This page explains the basics of how SolarKitGuide handles email, analytics, affiliate links, cookies, and visits to third-party retailer sites.

Information you provide

SolarKitGuide does not require account creation. If you email us, we may receive your email address, name, and whatever information you choose to include in your message. We use that information to respond, improve the site, handle corrections, or evaluate relevant partnership inquiries.

Please do not send sensitive personal, financial, utility-account, or electrical-system information unless you are comfortable sharing it by email.

Calculator information

The calculators are meant to help estimate solar panel wattage, battery capacity, inverter size, and related planning ranges. Inputs such as electric bill estimates, appliance loads, sun hours, and backup goals are used to produce on-page results. SolarKitGuide does not require you to create an account or submit those calculator inputs just to receive a result.

Analytics and site performance

SolarKitGuide may use basic analytics or hosting logs to understand page visits, traffic sources, device/browser information, errors, and site performance. This helps us improve calculators, guides, navigation, and content. If material analytics tools are in use, this policy is where we should say so.

Affiliate links, cookies, and retailers

SolarKitGuide may link to solar equipment retailers, affiliate networks, and product pages. If you click an affiliate link, the retailer or affiliate network may use cookies, tracking links, or similar technologies to credit SolarKitGuide for a referral. This does not change your price.

Once you click through to a retailer or affiliate network, you are on their systems and subject to their privacy policies, cookie practices, checkout systems, warranties, return rules, and customer-service processes. It is worth reviewing their terms and privacy policy before purchasing.

Third-party links

SolarKitGuide may link to retailers, manufacturer documentation, calculators, videos, technical resources, affiliate networks, and other websites. We are not responsible for the content, privacy practices, security, or policies of third-party websites once you leave SolarKitGuide.

Children’s privacy

SolarKitGuide is intended for a general audience interested in solar planning and equipment research. It is not directed toward children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
