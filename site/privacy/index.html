Privacy Policy

This page explains the basics of how SolarKitGuide handles information, affiliate links, cookies, and third-party services.

SolarKitGuide does not require account creation. If you email us, we may receive your email address, name, and whatever information you choose to include in your message. We use that information to respond, improve the site, handle corrections, or evaluate relevant partnership inquiries.

Do not send sensitive personal, financial, utility-account, or electrical-system information unless you are comfortable sharing it by email.

Calculator information

The calculators are intended to help estimate solar panel wattage, battery capacity, inverter size, and related planning ranges. SolarKitGuide does not require you to submit those calculator inputs to create an account or receive a result.
