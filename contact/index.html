SolarKitGuide is an educational resource. We can explain general sizing concepts, calculator assumptions, article topics, and what kind of gear category may fit a use case, but we cannot approve a final electrical design. For wiring, permitting, utility interconnection, roof mounting, battery safety, or code compliance, work with a qualified electrician, installer, engineer, or local authority.

If you have not used the calculator yet, start there first — it usually makes the next question much more specific and useful.
