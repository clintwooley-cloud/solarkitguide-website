Battery sizing

Battery Bank Calculator

Battery size is usually where undersized solar systems fail. You need enough usable energy, not just a big number on the battery label.

Estimate battery size

Simple battery formula

Battery kWh needed = daily kWh × days of autonomy ÷ usable depth of discharge.

LiFePO₄ batteries commonly allow deeper usable discharge than lead-acid or AGM batteries, which means a smaller rated bank can provide the same usable energy.

kWh vs amp-hours

kWh is usually clearer for comparing systems. Amp-hours only make sense when you also know voltage. A 100Ah battery at 12V is very different from 100Ah at 48V.
